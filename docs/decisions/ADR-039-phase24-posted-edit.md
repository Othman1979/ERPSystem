# ADR-039: Phase 24 — direct edit of posted entries and vouchers before year close

- **Status:** accepted (owner decision 2026-11-20: "direct edit without reversal", over the proposed
  reverse-and-repost and unpost-to-draft options)
- **Supersedes:** BUILD_PLAN §19 rule 4 and the CLAUDE.md rule "no update on POSTED", for the documents below only.

## Context

Corrections were reversal-only: a wrong posted journal entry, receipt or payment had to be reversed and
entered again under a new number. The owner wants to edit them directly while the year is open.

## Decision

1. **Scope.** Manual journal entries (`source_type = MANUAL`), receipts and payments (with their cheque).
   Opening, closing and reversal entries, and entries generated by other documents, are not edited here.
2. **In place.** Same entry/voucher number and id. `PostingService.amendEntry` locks the entry, re-validates
   and re-balances the new lines, replaces `journal_lines` and updates date, period, description and totals.
   Accounting effects still go only through `PostingService`.
3. **Guards.** Both the old and the new date must be in an OPEN period (closed period or year: `PERIOD_CLOSED`).
   Refused when: the entry is reversed (`ENTRY_ALREADY_REVERSED`), it is a reversal or closing entry, any of
   its lines is matched in a bank reconciliation, the voucher has active allocations
   (`VOUCHER_HAS_ALLOCATIONS`), or its cheque has moved past `IN_HAND`/`ISSUED` (`CHEQUE_INVALID_STATUS`).
4. **What changes on a voucher:** date, amount, cash/bank account (not for a cheque voucher), expense account
   (payment without party), cost centre, reference and description. The party stays. The cheque gets the
   new amount, and its RECEIVE/ISSUE event the new date.
5. **Audit.** Every edit writes `journal.amend`, `receipt.amend` or `payment.amend` with before/after.
6. **API.** `POST /journal-entries/:id/amend`, `POST /receipts/:id/amend`, `POST /payments/:id/amend`,
   `Idempotency-Key` required, permissions `journal.post` / `treasury.manage`.

## Deferred

Sales and purchase invoices: an edit changes stock movements and the cost of every later movement
(retroactive re-costing, §20 item 8), and an invoice sent to JoFotara is corrected by a credit note only.
They need their own decision (next PR).
